<h1 style="color: blue;"> The Importance of Frameworks </h1>
<p> UI frameworks matter because they make webpage building easy. Rather than writing long lines of CSS and HTML, you can import Bootstrap into your HTML and use the classes to style buttons, build layouts that apply to every device, and add components like navbars and footers in just a few lines. </p>

<!-- Paragraph 2 -->
<h1 style="color: blue;"> Development Without Frameworks </h1>
<p> Without UI frameworks, you're limited to just HTML and CSS. Although they're powerful building elements like navigation bars and footers would be a challenge. Creating things from scratch take a whole lot of time. It's also harder to expand your website easily because every new page means writing, testing, debugging, and styling more custom code which is hard for modularity. </p>

<!-- Paragraph 3 -->
<h1 style="color: blue;"> UI Frameworks Learning Curve </h1>
<p> Personally, I struggled at first with learning Bootstrap. There's a whole bunch of classes and utilities available where it was a little overwhelming and confusing to figure out what to use. For example, when creating a navbar, there's options like .navbar-expand-lg, .navbar-brand, .navbar-light, .dropdown-menu, etc. I personally have to go through every single different one and play around with it for a bit to just get a little bit more understanding. Once I get used to the formatting, I can easily edit the navbar and put what I want inside of it. Same goes for everything </p>
